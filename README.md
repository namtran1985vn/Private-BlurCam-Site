## About Private BlurCam

Private BlurCam is an iOS camera app that uses on-device processing to:
- Automatically detect and blur faces in real time while recording
- Remove metadata before sharing
- Modify voice pitch for additional privacy
- Record in resolutions from 480p to 4K

**Key Privacy Promise:** Camera media processing happens locally on your device. The app does not upload photos or videos for AI processing.
